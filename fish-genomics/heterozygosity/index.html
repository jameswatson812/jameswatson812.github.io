How it was computed

  • Fish. The full-QC arm: 262 fish with mean depth of at least 6×, genotypes PASS with depth ≥ 6 and GQ ≥ 20.
  • Sites. Silent (synonymous) sites of the high-quality set: outside repeats and low-mappability regions, callable in at least 236 of the 262 fish, mean depth ≤ 28×. Silent sites are the near-neutral reference class of this project.
  • Rate. Heterozygous genotypes divided by the fish's callable silent sites.
  • Era. The sampling year from the specimen manifest, split at 2005.

Data

  • Per-fish heterozygosity by unit and by subunit (TSV): sample code, year, era, unit, subunit, site, heterozygous sites, callable sites, rate.
