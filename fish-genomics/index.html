Fish genomics

Population genomics of the tidewater goby (Eucyclogobius newberryi), a small estuarine fish endemic to California coastal lagoons and listed under the U.S. Endangered Species Act. Its populations are subdivided among fragmented lagoon habitats, which makes it a natural system for asking how differentiation is distributed along the genome.

Genome-wide FST scan in windows of 101 SNPs

Following Maruki, Ye & Lynch (2022), Weir & Cockerham's θ among the five northern coastal units is averaged over non-overlapping windows of 101 consecutive SNPs with metapopulation minor-allele frequency ≥ 0.1: 1,010 windows, mean window FST 0.669 (SD 0.103). Outliers, in red, are windows whose FST exceeds the empirical distribution of their own 0.05-wide FST bin at a Bonferroni-adjusted t cutoff that also accounts for each window's jackknife sampling variance: 3 windows, holding 16 genes. The red line is the lowest outlier FST (0.911).

SNPs and windows used here

  • Samples. 262 fish sequenced to a mean depth of at least 6×. The metapopulation is the 233 fish of the five northern units (North Coast 71, Greater Bay Area 20, Central Coast 30, Conception 80, LA/Ventura 32); the South Coast unit (29 fish, E. kefauveri) is excluded.
  • Sites. Biallelic SNPs at high-quality sites: outside repeats and low-mappability regions, genotypes PASS with depth ≥ 6 and GQ ≥ 20, callable in at least 236 of the 262 fish, mean depth ≤ 28× to exclude collapsed paralogs. 1,739,987 SNPs in total, 1,422,005 of them polymorphic within the metapopulation.
  • Frequency filter. Metapopulation minor-allele frequency ≥ 0.1, with genotypes in at least 4 of the 5 units: 103,251 SNPs (7% of the polymorphic set).
  • Windows. Non-overlapping blocks of 101 consecutive qualifying SNPs along each scaffold, incomplete final blocks dropped: 1,010 windows covering 102,010 SNPs, median span 0.8 Mb. Window FST = Σa / Σ(a + b + c) over its SNPs; the window sampling variance is a delete-one jackknife over the SNPs.

Data

  • All windows (TSV): scaffold, start, end, FST, jackknife variance, t, bin cutoff, outlier flag and genes for each of the 1,010 windows.
  • Outlier windows (TSV).

Window design and outlier rule after Maruki, Ye & Lynch (2022), Mol. Biol. Evol. 39:msac152. Figures rendered with plotly for R.
