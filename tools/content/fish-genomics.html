Fish genomics

{fish_subnav}

Population genomics of the tidewater goby (Eucyclogobius newberryi), a small estuarine fish endemic to California coastal lagoons and listed under the U.S. Endangered Species Act. Its populations are subdivided among fragmented lagoon habitats, which makes it a natural system for asking how differentiation is distributed along the genome.

Genome-wide FST scan in windows of 101 SNPs

Following Maruki, Ye & Lynch (2022), Weir & Cockerham's θ among the five northern coastal units is averaged over non-overlapping windows of 101 consecutive SNPs with metapopulation minor-allele frequency ≥ 0.1: {n_windows} windows, mean window FST {mean} (SD {sd}). Outliers, in red, are windows whose FST exceeds the empirical distribution of their own 0.05-wide FST bin at a Bonferroni-adjusted t cutoff that also accounts for each window's jackknife sampling variance: {n_outliers} windows, holding {n_outlier_genes} genes. The red line is the lowest outlier FST ({cut}).

Pairwise scans between coastal units

The same window design applied to each of the {pw_n_pairs} pairs of the six coastal units, South Coast included (a between-species comparison, E. kefauveri against E. newberryi). For each pair the SNPs are those with at least 5 fish genotyped in both units and a minor-allele frequency ≥ 0.1 in the pair's pooled allele counts, so every pair has its own SNP set and window boundaries; outliers are called within each pair by the same bin-wise rule. The scan is available for three site classes: all high-quality SNP classes together (the design above), replacement SNPs only, and silent SNPs only. The restricted classes hold far fewer qualifying SNPs, so their windows are few and span tens of megabases; read them as class-specific genome-wide contrasts. The matrix gives the genome-wide FST between units as the ratio of summed variance components over the selected class.

SNPs and windows used here

  • Samples. 262 fish sequenced to a mean depth of at least 6×. The metapopulation is the 233 fish of the five northern units (North Coast 71, Greater Bay Area 20, Central Coast 30, Conception 80, LA/Ventura 32); the South Coast unit (29 fish, E. kefauveri) is excluded.
  • Sites. Biallelic SNPs at high-quality sites: outside repeats and low-mappability regions, genotypes PASS with depth ≥ 6 and GQ ≥ 20, callable in at least 236 of the 262 fish, mean depth ≤ 28× to exclude collapsed paralogs. {n_total} SNPs in total, {n_poly} of them polymorphic within the metapopulation.
  • Frequency filter. Metapopulation minor-allele frequency ≥ 0.1, with genotypes in at least 4 of the 5 units: {n_snps_maf10} SNPs ({pct_maf10} of the polymorphic set).
  • Windows. Non-overlapping blocks of 101 consecutive qualifying SNPs along each scaffold, incomplete final blocks dropped: {n_windows} windows covering {n_snps_in_windows} SNPs, median span {span_mb} Mb. Window FST = Σa / Σ(a + b + c) over its SNPs; the window sampling variance is a delete-one jackknife over the SNPs.
  • Pairwise scans. The same window rule for each of the {pw_n_pairs} pairs of units, with the frequency filter computed on the pair's pooled allele counts and both units required to have at least 5 fish genotyped; run on all SNP classes, on replacement SNPs only and on silent SNPs only. SNP and window counts per pair and class are in the tables above.

Data

  • All windows (TSV): scaffold, start, end, FST, jackknife variance, t, bin cutoff, outlier flag and genes for each of the {n_windows} windows.
  • Outlier windows (TSV).
  • Pairwise windows, per-pair summary, outlier windows and the FST matrix (TSV) for each site class: all SNP classes (windows, summary, outliers, matrix), replacement (summary, outliers, matrix), silent (summary, outliers, matrix).
